Eric and the Hidden Principles of Mana
Purpose
This is an author-facing reference for the deeper principles that may
inform Eric's discoveries. These ideas should not be explained to
readers all at once. They should emerge through observation,
experimentation and story events.
1. The Difference in Approach
The conventional magical framework primarily studies:

Mana → World

Practitioners investigate how mana can be shaped and manipulated to
produce effects in the world.

Eric considers:

World ↔ Mana

He investigates how physical conditions, materials and processes
influence mana, as well as how mana influences the physical world.

The difference is not that conventional mages are unintelligent or that
their methods do not work. Their traditions have developed effective
practical techniques, but their explanations tend to focus on mana's
influence on the world.

Eric asks whether the physical world can also be used to understand,
guide or assist mana.
2. Mana and Physical Principles
The underlying author model takes inspiration from physical particle
behaviour:
- Pure mana is the default, neutral state.
- Mana can behave in different states.
- Fire-oriented mana is associated with rapidly vibrating particles.
- Cold-oriented mana is associated with slower-moving particles.
- Changing mana from one state to another requires energy and can
  cause conversion loss.
- The state from which mana begins affects the difficulty of changing
  it. Pure mana may be easier to convert into a target state than mana
  already in a different state.
These are retained as deeper worldbuilding principles. They do not need
to be presented as established scientific knowledge within the world's
magical institutions.
3. Affinity Reinterpreted
The people of the world use the word affinity for a mage's developed
ability to produce or handle a particular mana state efficiently.

The deeper interpretation is that the mage has become more efficient at
changing mana into that state, with less conversion loss.

Affinity is developed through environmental exposure and deliberate
practice, not necessarily an innate elemental identity.
4. The Physical World Influences Mana
Environmental mana has varying density and characteristics. The physical
environment influences the local mana.

For example, cold surroundings may have mana characteristics that make a
fire effect harder to sustain. Warm or fire-supportive surroundings may
help sustain it.

A magical fire can continue after its caster stops actively channeling
by drawing on environmental mana. It disappears when the mana supporting
the effect is depleted or unavailable in sufficient quantity.

A real physical fire is different: combustion can continue through fuel
and oxygen without environmental mana sustaining the combustion itself.
Magic used to assist that fire still depends on mana.
5. Eric's Physical-Process Approach
Eric may learn to use physical processes and materials to reduce the
amount of work that mana must perform.

For example, rather than producing an entire fire effect through mana,
he could use flint and steel to create a spark, start real combustion
with suitable fuel and oxygen, and use a smaller amount of mana to
assist ignition, heat, airflow or flame spread.

The physical fire can then continue through ordinary combustion. This is
not automatically stronger or more convenient than conventional magic.
It may require preparation, materials and suitable conditions.

The broader principle is that Eric asks which parts of an effect truly
require mana and which parts can be accomplished by the physical world.
6. How to Reveal This in the Story
- Do not have Eric immediately understand the complete nature of mana.
- Let him first learn the world's conventional explanations and
  techniques.
- Let observations create questions rather than instant answers.
- Have him test ideas, make mistakes and revise his assumptions.
- Let established magic remain useful and effective.
- Avoid making every conventional mage ignorant or foolish.
- Reveal deeper principles through concrete problems, experiments and
  consequences rather than long lectures.
7. Open Details
- The exact mechanism behind mana states and conversion.
- How Eric first recognises the relationship between physical
  conditions and mana.
- Which discoveries he makes first and what evidence leads him to
  them.
- The limits, costs and failure modes of his physical-process
  approach.
- How conventional practitioners respond when confronted with
  techniques that challenge their assumptions.
