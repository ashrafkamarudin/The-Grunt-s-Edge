# Currency

## Exchange Rates
- 100 copper = 1 silver
- 100 silver = 1 gold

## Daily Costs (rough estimates for a commoner)
- Loaf of bread: 2-3 copper
- Bowl of stew at a tavern: 5-6 copper
- Mug of ale: 2-3 copper
- Cheap room for the night: 8-10 copper

## Monthly Costs (single person, frugal)
- Food: ~1.5-2 silver
- Incidentals (clothing, tools, minor needs): ~0.5-1 silver
- **Total: ~2-3 silver per month**

## Wages
- Common laborer: ~5-10 copper per day (~1.5-3 silver per month)
- Levy soldier: 3 silver per month (paid after campaign ends — food and shelter provided separately)
- Death sum for levy: 10 silver (paid to listed family upon death)

## Military Pay Notes
- Levies are paid only after the campaign concludes, not monthly
- This means they serve the entire campaign without seeing any money — the salary is a promise, not a living wage
- Food and shelter are provided separately, so the salary is pure disposable income
- Sworn soldiers and veterans earn more than levies
- Death sums are small enough to show the system's indifference — 10 silver is not compensation, it's a token
