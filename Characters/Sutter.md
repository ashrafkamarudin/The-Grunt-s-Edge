# Sutter

## Basic Information
- **Age:** Late 30s or early 40s
- **From:** Unknown — hasn't talked about where he came from
- **Background:** Unknown — longest-serving member of the squad
- **Time at Fort Lowen:** Longer than anyone else in the tent

## Personality
- Speaks rarely, but when he does it lands. His words carry weight because he doesn't waste them.
- Calm, grounded. Not a leader — an anchor. The squad doesn't look to him for orders, but they settle when he speaks.
- Dry humor — unexpected, observational. *"Your bread was too honest"* delivered flat enough that it takes a second to land.
- Doesn't correct or instruct. States what he sees and lets others act on it. *"We should sleep"* — not a suggestion, just an observation that the conversation is over.
- Not warm, not cold. Present.

## Voice
- Short. Observational. Flat delivery that makes his dry humor land harder.
- Doesn't explain. Doesn't repeat himself. If no one acts on what he said, that's their choice.

## Notes
- Stretches in the morning — takes care of his body, knows how to pace himself
- First to invite Eric and Darrin to morning training — not hospitality, just recognizing they needed direction
- The line *"Same to you"* when Mila says *"Try not to die tomorrow"* — the most he'll concede to warmth
- Says *"Too stubborn to die"* about Mila — knows his squadmates well enough to summarize them in a single sentence
- Has been here the longest — if anyone knows the fort's rhythm, it's him
