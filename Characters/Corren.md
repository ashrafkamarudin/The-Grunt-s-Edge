# Corren

## Basic Information
- **Age:** Late 30s
- **Role:** Field medic / healer
- **Rank:** Low-rank Support Corps — technically in the military hierarchy, but without rank or patronage to demand proper resources
- **Origin:** Small town in the Valorian Empire. Son of a carpenter. Conscripted years ago and never left.
- **Affinity:** Life affinity — enough to use healing magic multiple times a day, not enough to heal everyone. Has to triage who gets magic and who gets cloth and pressure.

## Personality
- Speaks like a tired craftsman, not a soldier. He talks about wounds the way a carpenter talks about wood — practical, no drama, just what needs to be done.
- Doesn't give orders the way officers do. He tells you what he needs and assumes you'll handle it because there's work to do.
- Has a faint trace of dry humor, the kind that comes from seeing too much without being able to stop it.
- Not cold — just worn down. He doesn't have energy left for anything except the next patient.
- Judges people by their hands, not their words — he watches what you do, not what you say.
- Doesn't treat Eric like a soldier. He treats him like a person who happens to be in a tent helping.

## Voice Tone
- "Cloth behind you. Sack on the left." — not an order, just a direction
- "If you hold it like that your hand will cramp. Try shifting your grip." — advice, not correction
- "You're not useless." — not praise, just an observation
- "I could use another pair of hands tomorrow. If you're not busy dying." — the closest he gets to warmth

## Notes
- Was a carpenter's son before conscription. The army found he had enough Life affinity to be useful in the medical tent and sent him there instead of the line.
- Years of doing this alone have worn down his patience for anything that doesn't directly help patients.
- Heals Eric's ribs not out of kindness but because Eric proved useful and he'd rather have a functioning assistant tomorrow.
- His magic visibly drains him — after healing, Eric feels heavier, not better.
