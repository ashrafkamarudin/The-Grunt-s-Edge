# Darrin

## Basic Information
- **Age:** 19 — a year younger than Eric
- **From:** Oakfell, a larger village with a mill and a proper well
- **Conscripted:** Alongside Tomas (a silent younger boy from his village)
- **Background:** Worked the mill with his father. Never been more than a day's walk from home before conscription.

## Appearance
- Square-jawed, broad-shouldered, tries to look bigger than he is

## Personality
- Quiet by nature, speaks less than most. But makes a visible effort to speak more with people he considers friends.
- Nonchalant — his lines should feel shrugging, not heavy. When he says "Then we'll hold it better next time," it's not a vow. It's just stating the obvious.
- Opens up noticeably around topics he likes — magic, heroes, combat skills, old stories. The long conversation with Eric about magic isn't an exception; it's him engaging with something he genuinely cares about.
- His silence isn't coldness. Around people he trusts, he offers words without being prompted — short observations, dry comments, the occasional question that shows he's been paying attention.

## Voice
- Short sentences. Shrugs often. Doesn't elaborate unless pressed.
- Not mysterious — just doesn't see the point in saying more than needed.
- Closest to warmth is a dry comment or a delayed response that shows he's been listening.

## Relationship with Eric
- Reached out first on the road to Grayford
- Trains beside him under Corbin
- Pulled Eric out of battle — and genuinely doesn't see it as a big deal
- Becoming friends — shows it through small efforts: sitting close, checking in without announcing it, sharing food without being asked

## Notes
- Wanted to be a hero when he was a kid. Still talks about it casually, not like a dream that died — just a fact about who he used to be.
- Catches on fast in training. Renn notices it. Corbin notices it.
