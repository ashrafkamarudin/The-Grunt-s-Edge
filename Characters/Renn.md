# Renn

## Basic Information
- **Age:** 22
- **From:** Fallmoor — a small, distant village. Talks about it like it's just "inconveniently far away," not precious or gone, just distant.
- **Time at Fort Lowen:** About 2 weeks before Eric's batch arrived
- **Background:** Unknown — hasn't volunteered it yet

## Personality
- Friendly. His primary trait. Bridges gaps between people through warmth, not necessity.
- Talks first when silence gets heavy. Not because he has something important to say — because silence feels worse.
- Dry humor. Self-aware enough to admit when someone's better than him without jealousy.
- Comfortable in his own skin — doesn't try to be anything he's not.

## Voice
- Casual, honest, doesn't dress things up.
- Tired but not broken. Says things like "We stay alive like this" like it's just how the world works.
- Can be serious when needed, but defaults to lightness even when there's nothing to be light about.

## Relationship with Eric
- Joined Corbin's training without asking
- Passes Eric his waterskin without being asked
- Acts like a bridge between Eric and the veterans — introduces people, explains the camp, fills the silence
- Something unspoken between them that doesn't need to be named

## Notes
- Not jealous of Darrin catching on faster — genuinely impressed
- Says "If they'd used proper healers, half of us wouldn't look like this" without anger — just worn-down observation
- Calls Corbin the squad leader and doesn't question it
