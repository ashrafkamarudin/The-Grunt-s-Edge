# Mila

## Basic Information
- **Age:** Early 20s
- **From:** Unknown — deflected when Renn asked during the first night
- **Background:** Unknown — not volunteered yet
- **Role in the squad:** The one who breaks tension. Keeps morale from sinking completely.

## Personality
- Playful, teasing, deflecting. Uses humor to keep the dark at arm's length.
- Described by Renn as "doesn't talk much" on first impression. By Chapter 9, she's one of the most present voices in the tent — her humor comes out once she's comfortable.
- Practical under pressure — laces her boots at dawn before anyone asks. When the horn sounds, she kicks Eric's boot to jolt him into action without waiting for orders.
- Quieter at night. Exhaustion strips the humor away and leaves something honest underneath.
- Her laughter isn't fake. But it's chosen — a decision not to let the dark win.

## Voice
- Playful, singsong, deflecting. *"I told the monsters I had laundry to do."*
- Can go sharp in a heartbeat — teases Renn, shoots back without hesitation, laughs with the rest.
- At night, she softens. Words slurring: *"Try not to die tomorrow."*

## Notes
- Laces her boots at dawn — practical, self-sufficient
- In the horn scene, she's already moving before Eric registers the sound
- Her humor is self-protective but not dishonest — she genuinely finds things to laugh about
